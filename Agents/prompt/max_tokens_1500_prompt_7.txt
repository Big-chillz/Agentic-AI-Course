Ah, Delhi! Where the traffic is slower than a government file, the weather has more mood swings than a Bollywood heroine, and street food is basically a dare from your stomach. Let’s roast this glorious chaos with love—here are the 5 best Delhi-themed jokes, served hot with a side of chutney:

---

**1. On Delhi Weather:**  
*"Delhi doesn’t have seasons—it has survival levels. Summer: ‘Why did I leave the AC?’ Monsoon: ‘Why did I wear clothes?’ Winter: ‘Is this Delhi or did someone accidentally teleport us to Shimla?’ And spring? That’s just a rumor spread by people who own sweaters."*

**2. On Traffic Jams:**  
*"Stuck in Delhi traffic? Don’t call it a jam—call it ‘forced family time.’ You’ll bond with the auto guy yelling at the scooter, the auntie doing her makeup in the rearview mirror, and that one biker who thinks he’s in a Fast & Furious sequel… all while going 0 km/h. Pro tip: Keep snacks handy. You might be there till the next census."*

**3. On Street Food Courage:**  
*"Eating golgappa in Delhi isn’t a snack—it’s a fear factor episode. You watch the guy dip the puri in water that looks like it survived a monsoon flood, then you eat it… and somehow, you feel *more* alive. That’s not immunity—that’s Delhi street food vaccine. Bonus round: Repeat after me—‘Just one more plate…’ (said while clutching your stomach)."*

**4. On Auto-Rickshaw Drivers:**  
*"Delhi autos don’t have meters—they have mood meters. ‘Sahab, petrol khatam ho gaya’ (even though you just filled it), ‘Route bandh hai’ (while the road is empty), and the classic ‘Meter nahi chalta’… which really means ‘My mood doesn’t chalta.’ Pro tip: Always agree on the price… then double it in your head. It’s cheaper than therapy."*

**5. On ‘Delhi Time’:**  
*"In Delhi, ‘I’m five minutes away’ is the most optimistic lie since ‘I’ll start dieting tomorrow.’ You say it while still brushing your teeth, then leave 20 minutes later… but you’ll honk twice when you’re nearby to pretend you’re punctual. Honesty? Overrated. Punctuality? A colonial concept. We run on IST—‘Indian Stretchable Time’—and Delhi’s the elastic capital."*
